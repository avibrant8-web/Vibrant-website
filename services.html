Services

Six services, one responsible contractor

Mechanical, electrical and building works delivered under one point of coordination.

Mechanical Contracting

Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of mechanical systems for commercial, residential, institutional and public buildings.

  • HVAC and ventilation systems
  • Chilled water and pipework
  • Plumbing and drainage
  • Fire fighting systems

Building Project Contracting

Delivery of all kinds of building projects, with coordinated management of trades, programme, quality and safety from mobilisation to handover.

  • Residential, commercial and institutional buildings
  • Extensions, fit-out and refurbishment
  • Planning and site management
  • Handover and documentation

Electrical Contracting

Complete electrical installations from the incoming supply to the final circuit, built and tested to the approved design and applicable codes.

  • Low-voltage distribution and switchgear
  • Containment, cabling and earthing
  • Lighting and small power
  • Testing and energisation

Electromechanical Equipment Installation and Maintenance

Installation, connection and upkeep of the electromechanical equipment that keeps a building running.

  • Pumps, generators, chillers and air handling units
  • Positioning, alignment and connection
  • Planned preventive maintenance
  • Corrective and breakdown support

Electrical Wiring and Fixtures Installation

Neat, safe and compliant installation of wiring and fixtures for new works and upgrades.

  • Concealed and surface wiring
  • Lighting fixtures, switches and sockets
  • Distribution boards and protection
  • Inspection and testing to standard

Building Maintenance

Keeping buildings safe, functional and presentable through planned and responsive maintenance.

  • Planned preventive maintenance
  • Corrective repairs
  • MEP and general building upkeep
  • Renovation and refurbishment

Licence note — please read

Three of the services above — Mechanical Contracting, Building Project Contracting and Electrical Contracting — are marked on the trade licence as requiring a classification certificate from the Department of Urban Planning and Municipalities. They are therefore not described here as unconditionally licensed. The remaining three services carry no such mark on the licence.

No vendor or membership affiliations are claimed on this website.
