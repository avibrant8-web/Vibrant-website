HSE & Quality

Engineered for quality. Managed for safety.

Every project runs to a project quality plan and a project HSE plan, with inspection and test plans and permit controls on every site.

Quality Assurance / Quality Control

Project Quality Plan VEM-QC-PP-001

The Quality Assurance / Quality Control (QA/QC) Project Plan, document VEM-QC-PP-001 (Revision 00, October 2026), is the principal quality management document for each project. It sets a clear, effective system for controlling the quality of MEP works: quality policies and objectives, established procedures, assigned duties and authority, and inspection, testing and audit programmes to verify that required standards are met. QA/QC activities are planned in advance, including records and approvals, before the associated work starts.

Its scope runs from kick-off to final completion and handover, covering project management, planning, engineering coordination and shop drawings; procurement, subcontracting, delivery, receiving and storage; installation of electrical, HVAC, plumbing and drainage, fire fighting, fire alarm and extra-low-voltage systems; and testing, commissioning, handover, documentation and the defects liability period. Major sub-contractors and suppliers must comply with the quality requirements for their scope. The plan is prepared to meet the contract documents and the principles of ISO 9001:2015.

How quality is controlled

  • Controlled project documentation and numbering system; control of quality records.
  • Material receiving inspection against purchase order, delivery note and approved submittal.
  • Inspection and test plans (ITPs) with hold and witness points by discipline.
  • Calibrated test instruments with traceable certificates; out-of-calibration equipment removed from use.
  • Control of non-conforming work (NCRs with root cause, corrective and preventive action, agreed closure periods).
  • Internal audits at planned intervals and monthly quality reporting.

Quality objectives and targets

  • Material and work inspections approved at first submission — 90% or better.
  • Milestones met on programme.
  • Zero open NCRs past their closure date.
  • Rejected submittals resubmitted within 7 working days — 100%.
  • Test and measuring equipment in calibration when used — 100%.
  • Operatives and supervisors trained or briefed before starting — 100%.
  • No unresolved client complaint at milestones or handover.

The QA/QC Engineer establishes, implements and maintains the quality management system on the project and has authority to stop work or reject material that does not conform.

Health, Safety & Environment

HSE Project Plan VEM-HSE-PP-001

The Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) Project Plan, document VEM-HSE-PP-001 (Revision 00, October 2026), is written to align with ISO 45001:2018 principles and with UAE and Abu Dhabi laws, regulations, codes and standards plus applicable international standards named in the specification.

Policy and objectives

  • Eliminate occupational injuries and illnesses and achieve zero incidents.
  • Meet every statutory HSE requirement and strive to exceed client expectations.
  • Ensure all personnel are competent for their assigned tasks.
  • Ensure safety concerns can be raised at every level.
  • Achieve zero environmental incidents.
  • Ensure every employee returns home at the end of the working day in sound physical condition.

A signed copy of the policy is displayed in site offices and welfare facilities and presented at every worker's site HSE induction.

Measurable targets

  • Fatalities: 0 · Lost Time Injuries: 0 · Environmental incidents (spills, unauthorised discharge): 0.
  • HSE induction completed before site access: 100%.
  • Toolbox talks weekly, all crews.
  • Corrective actions closed by agreed date: 100%.
  • Emergency drills at least one per quarter. Reviewed monthly (drills quarterly, induction and toolbox talks weekly).

Core controls

  • Defined HSE roles, job hazard analysis (JHA) and a permit-to-work (PTW) system.
  • Induction plus job-specific training and competency certification.
  • Heat-stress prevention, including the UAE summer midday work ban (no outdoor work 12:30–15:00 during the period set by UAE regulation), shaded rest areas and cool water.
  • Noise, respiratory, hazardous-materials, water and sanitation, and smoking controls; PPE specific to the hazard.
  • Ladders, cranes and lifting, housekeeping and manual-handling rules.
  • First-aid, medical transport, evacuation and emergency procedures.
  • Waste segregation, spill prevention and responsible disposal.
  • Full investigation of all accidents and near-misses with formal incident reporting.
