About Us

A UAE contractor for mechanical, electrical and building works

Vibrant Electromechanical Contracting LLC is a UAE-based contractor delivering mechanical, electrical and building works for government, institutional, commercial and residential clients.

Vibrant at a glance

Planned, built, tested and handed over — to the specification

We combine in-house engineering coordination with disciplined site supervision, so every installation is planned, built, tested and handed over to the specification and on programme.

Our scope runs from new-build MEP packages for towers, schools, clinics and public facilities to long-term maintenance of the buildings we serve. Our project record runs from Abu Dhabi and Al Ain to Dubai and Sharjah — large-scale public infrastructure, education and healthcare facilities, residential and commercial towers, and private palaces.

We are proud that many of our clients return to us for their next project.

Our Commitment

Three simple promises

Build it right the first time

Approved materials, inspection and test plans, and hold and witness points on every project.

Send every person home safe

A project HSE plan, risk assessments, permit-to-work controls, trained supervisors and regular inspections.

Hand over a building that performs as intended

Testing and commissioning witnessed and recorded to the specification, with as-built documentation and training.

How We Deliver

Five stages, every project

  1. 1. PlanReview of contract, programme and resources; submittals and method statements prepared.
  2. 2. ProcureApproved materials and vendors; delivery programmed against site needs.
  3. 3. InstallSupervised installation to approved drawings, with inspections at each stage.
  4. 4. TestTesting and commissioning, witnessed and recorded to the specification.
  5. 5. HandoverAs-built drawings, manuals, warranties and training; support after handover.

Delivery is supported by planned programmes, early procurement of long-lead items, coordinated shop drawings and clear reporting — keeping work on time and on specification.
